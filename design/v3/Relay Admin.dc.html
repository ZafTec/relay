Tool lifecycle · confirmation dialogs

DEPRECATE

Deprecating image.generate.legacy v2 keeps it callable, marks it deprecated in the catalog, and requires a retirement date. Existing integrations keep working until then.

DISABLE

Disabling video.generate rejects new runs immediately with a typed error. Runs already in flight are allowed to finish. Reversible via Restore.

RETIRE

Retiring image.generate.v1 is permanent. It disappears from the catalog and can never be called again. Historical runs and artifacts remain readable.
