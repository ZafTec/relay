An artifact is a durable output owned by the workspace. A share link is a revocable Relay resource with its own expiry and policy — separate from the short-lived S3 signature Relay uses internally to move bytes. Revoking a share link blocks future Relay resolutions; it cannot retroactively invalidate a signature already handed out before revocation.
